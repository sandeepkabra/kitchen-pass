Privacy Policy — Kitchen Pass

Last updated: October 2026

Kitchen Pass is a pickleball scorekeeping and tournament management app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Account information

If you choose to sign in, Kitchen Pass uses Firebase Authentication (a Google service) with your email address and password. We don't see or store your password ourselves — Firebase handles that directly. You can also use the app entirely without an account ("Continue without an account"), in which case nothing about you is sent anywhere and all your data stays only on your device.

Tournament and match data

If you're signed in, the tournaments, teams, players, and match scores you create are stored in Firebase Firestore (also a Google service) so they sync across your devices and so tournament links can be followed live by others you choose to share them with. This includes player names you enter, scores, and match history.

DUPR integration

Kitchen Pass includes DUPR rating display for players. At present this uses simulated/mock test data only — no real connection to a DUPR account is made, and no data is sent to DUPR's servers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't run analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We don't collect data beyond what's described above.

Third-party services used

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage, when you choose to sign in.
  • SheetJS — processes Excel files you import, entirely in your browser; files are never uploaded anywhere.

Your choices

You can use Kitchen Pass without ever creating an account. If you've signed in and want your account data removed, contact the organizer/developer using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the app's developer, Sandeep Kabra, via the contact details on the Kitchen Pass tournament pages you've interacted with.
