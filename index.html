Sandeep Kabra
Creator of Kitchen Pass

Hi, I'm Sandeep, a passionate weekend pickleball player. Like many players, I often found myself losing track of the score, debating whose serve it was, and struggling to organize match days smoothly.

That experience inspired me to build Kitchen Pass, a simple and intuitive platform designed for pickleball players. It helps you keep accurate, rules-based scores, organize tournaments, manage match days, and focus on what matters most: enjoying the game with friends, all from your phone.
